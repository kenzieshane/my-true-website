Heliohost is... rough.

Posted on: May 22, 2026 Author: KenzieShane Category: Announcements

Helio host is an awesome web hosting provider, might even be the best one I knew about yet. As amazing as it is, The downtime of their services is even more amazing.

Before a certain event had occured, this hosting provider regularly restarts their servers to what seems like multiple times a day making my site inaccesible in many times. This only happens in the free tier, or Johnny servers.

Thankfully, the HelioHost team is friendly and active, giving compensations here and there, and helping others too.

Because Johnny is down while being rebuilt. We emailed all active Johnny accounts on May 9th give them a free Morty trial to keep their websites online during the rebuild. People who didn't accept the free trial or ignored our email will still have websites that are offline until the rebuild is complete like we said in #news

But i decided it's time to move on, temporarily.

Heliohost, is amazing for web experiments, laravel projects, and many more!, but for now... I prefer a stable a hosting provider. I wish to find a way in which to keep everything as accesible as I can, too. (That means without all of the worst tracking and bad stuff from InfinityFree too)

So at last, i don't have a backup of my original site, and now kinda stuck with having to rewrite my site as a static site and more...
